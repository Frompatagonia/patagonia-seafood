Especie pelágica con migraciones verticales diarias: asciende a media agua de noche y desciende cerca del fondo de día. Se desplaza formando grandes cardúmenes y se defiende expeliendo tinta. Pelagic species with daily vertical migrations: rises into mid-water at night and descends near the seabed by day. Moves in large schools and defends itself by ejecting ink.
